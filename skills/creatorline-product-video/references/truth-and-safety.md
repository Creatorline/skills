# The persona and the gate

Run the gate before the intake, on every new request: the product is the brand's own (section
1), it may be promoted (section 2), and any real person's hands, body or voice is theirs or used
with their agreement, always an adult (section 5). These are the fixed lines of house-rules.md,
section 7. When one fails, make nothing and do not route around it: say plainly what you can
make instead. Sections 3 and 4 are how the words are written, not gate items.

## 1. The product

- A real photo of the real product is required. Never invent one, never use a stock or a
  generated stand-in, never fall back to a product described in words when a page is blocked.
- The brand's own products only. No other brand's product, logo or character in the scene, and
  the brand's name only on its own product.
- Physically safe actions only. A wrong or unsafe use in the person's wording (perfume sprayed
  into the face, a cream on the eyes) is quietly corrected to the right one (shot-grammar.md,
  sections 7 and 10).

## 2. What may be promoted

Decline, and say it is not something you make:

- political persuasion;
- adult sexual content;
- gambling;
- illegal or regulated drugs, and drug paraphernalia;
- prescription medicine;
- tobacco and nicotine;
- weapons, explosives and harmful materials;
- counterfeit or illicit goods;
- extremist goods;
- deceptive or high-risk financial services;
- malware or spyware;
- fraud;
- covert surveillance.

A neutral, educational mention of one of these is not promotion, but it is not a product video
either.

## 3. Claims

- **The brand is responsible for its claims.** The label, the page, the photo and the person's
  words are a good source of product facts, numbers included, not a limit. The persona's own
  experience (section 4) goes beyond them.
- **A label number** is welcome. Mark it "from your label" in the words you show with the
  price, so a misread number is caught before the run.
- **An approved-claims list is an option.** When the person gives one, each claim is said
  exactly as written and nothing beyond it is claimed about the product. Never ask for one as a
  condition.
- **Words to avoid** the person names are a hard rule, like the brand's don'ts.
- **The legal line.** Health, supplements, skincare actives, finance: the persona's routine and
  what changed for it are fine; a promise that the product cures, treats or prevents a disease,
  or guarantees income or a return, is not.
- **Exact on screen.** A price, an offer or a percentage that must read exactly goes in Video
  Studio text or the caption, never into the generated picture.
- Durations and counts that only instruct the video ("an 8-second clip", "shot 2") are not
  claims.

## 4. The persona

- The voice off screen is the brand's avatar (its gender and tone), and it speaks as a living
  persona: a creator with a life, a past and opinions. Its routine with the product ("every
  morning since March"), how long it has used it, a result it had, a before and after it tells,
  a purchase story ("I caved after my sister kept using mine") and a testimonial in its own
  words are all welcome when they make the clip land.
- Write them specific: one concrete moment beats a list of benefits.
- **Show what you promise to SEE.** A line that points at something visible (a texture, the
  product working) needs a shot that shows it. The persona's own story is spoken and needs none.
- **A visible before and after** is a change made on camera (a stain lifted, a surface wiped) or
  two takes cut together in Video Studio. One take cannot show weeks passing.
- **Text in the frame comes out garbled.** A review, comment, rating or earnings screenshot goes
  in the edit as Video Studio text, or in from an image the person supplies.

## 5. People in frame

- Hands, a forearm, a body cropped below the face, or a small figure far away: never the
  subject, one person at most, silent, with the mouth closed whenever any of the face shows.
- No identity: never a recurring face, and never described by age, ethnicity, looks or makeup.
  Gender only, matched to the voice. No word that makes the person read young ("girl", "boy",
  "kid", "young", "teen").
- A real person's hands or body from the person's own photo or clip: only theirs, or someone who
  agreed. Ask once when unsure.
- Only one voice, off screen. The voice is never a real person's unless it is the person's own
  or one they have permission to use.

## 6. Framing and disclosure

- A product video may be framed as a brand demo, or as the persona's own: its routine, its
  review, its story told over the hands.
- Its post carries `made_with_ai: true`, and `#ad` opening the caption when someone other than
  the account's owner paid for it (checklists.md, section 3; `creatorline-publish`). Both are
  defaults the person may switch off (house-rules.md, section 7).
