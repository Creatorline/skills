# Shot lists by item

What to film for each kind of item: the look photo's pose, the shots for a 10 s and a 15 s clip,
the cues that show fabric and fit, safe lines, and what to look for in a real clip (Route B).

## 1. How to read these

- **Shots.** Every shot is 3 s or more; a 10 s clip holds 3 shots, 15 s holds 4 or 5. Write each
  shot as one plain line in the brief's `Beats:` line, in order. The seconds in the tables are
  for planning: leave them out of the brief, the server's writer times the shots.
- **Framing words.** Full body (head to feet), three-quarter (head to knee), waist-up, chest-up.
  Each shot opens on a different framing from the one before. The last shot is chest-up or wider.
- **The phone.** Full-body shots need the phone propped (on a dresser, a shelf, the floor for
  shoes). Write where it is. Keep mirrors out of the room.
- **Lines.** Opinions and what the frame shows. The sample lines are patterns: rewrite them
  from the item card's facts and the brand's voice. A shot with a move holds about 2 words
  fewer than the speech budget.
- **Look photo.** The pose and `aspect_ratio` for the `photo-edit` look. Default model:
  `2:3` for a full body, `3:4` for chest-up.
- **Swap clip.** What a real clip needs for Route B.

## 2. Tops and shirts

Sells on: the neckline, the sleeves, where it sits at the waist (cropped, tucked, oversized),
the drape.

Look photo: three-quarter or full body, `2:3`, plain bottoms named in the wardrobe line, arms a
little away from the sides.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Waist-up, already turning back to the phone, smoothing the hem. Hook line. (3 s) | Same (3 s) |
| 2 | Chest-up, a shoulder turned to the window so the neckline and sleeve catch the light. (4 s) | Full body: how it sits with the bottoms, a step toward the phone. (3 s) |
| 3 | Three-quarter, a step back, hands in pockets. Verdict. (3 s) | Chest-up on the neckline and sleeve. (3 s) |
| 4 | | Side turn: the length at the hip and the back. (3 s) |
| 5 | | Three-quarter, settled. Verdict. (3 s) |

- Fabric cues: a knit shifts in the light with each breath; poplin keeps a crisp collar; satin
  flashes on a turn; linen creases at the elbow.
- Lines: "Boxy fit, cropped right at the waist." "Look at the sleeve on this." "The kind of white
  tee that goes with everything."
- Watch: a print renders when it is big and simple; small lettering garbles. Tucked or untucked,
  jacket on or off: one state per shot, changed only across a cut.
- Swap clip: waist-up or full-body try-on in a top of a similar length, arms that move (adjusting a
  sleeve, a shrug).

## 3. Dresses and skirts

Sells on: the length, the waist, how the hem moves, the back.

Look photo: full body, feet in frame, `2:3`.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full body: steps back from the phone into view, the hem still swinging. Hook line. (3 s) | Same (3 s) |
| 2 | Full body: one slow turn, front to back to front, the skirt swinging. (4 s) | Full body: two steps toward the phone, the hem moving. (3 s) |
| 3 | Three-quarter, settled, hands relaxed. Verdict. (3 s) | Waist-up: the neckline, the waist seam, the buttons. (3 s) |
| 4 | | Full body: one slow turn to the back (the zip, the ties, the cut-out). (3 s) |
| 5 | | Three-quarter or seated on the bed edge. Verdict. (3 s) |

- Fabric cues: a flowing fabric shows in the hem on a step; a structured one holds its shape on
  the turn.
- Lines: "Midi length, right at mid-calf." "The back is the best part." "This is what I'd wear to
  a garden wedding."
- Watch: one twirl at most. Put the length in the wardrobe line so it stays the same in every
  shot. A strapless or backless cut is said as it is: "strapless neckline", "open back".
- Swap clip: a twirl or walk-in dress try-on, in the same length range (mini, midi, maxi).

## 4. Trousers and jeans

Sells on: the rise, the leg shape, the length at the ankle, the movement.

Look photo: full body, `2:3`, a plain top and the shoes named in the wardrobe line.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full body: walks toward the propped phone. Hook line. (3 s) | Same (3 s) |
| 2 | Three-quarter from the side: turns to show the rise and the leg line. (4 s) | Three-quarter side turn. (3 s) |
| 3 | Full body, hands in the pockets. Verdict. (3 s) | Phone low: the hem over the shoe as they step. (3 s) |
| 4 | | Full body from the back. (3 s) |
| 5 | | Full body, settled. Verdict. (3 s) |

- Fabric cues: the denim's fade shows as the knee bends; a wide leg swings with a step; a
  tailored crease stays sharp.
- Lines: "High rise, wide leg, cropped at the ankle." "Watch how these move."
- Watch: no body words ("slims", "lifts", "snatched"); the top stays the same in every shot.
- Swap clip: full-body walking clips; a jeans try-on with turns.

## 5. Outerwear

Sells on: the length, the shoulders, the closure, the collar, how it layers.

Look photo: full body, `2:3`, worn open or closed as it is usually worn (one state, in the
wardrobe line).

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full body: walks into view already wearing it, lifting the collar. Hook line. (3 s) | Same (3 s) |
| 2 | Waist-up: the closure and the fabric, hands at the pockets. (4 s) | Waist-up on the closure. (3 s) |
| 3 | Full body: turns to the back, then a look over the shoulder. Verdict. (3 s) | Three-quarter: hands in the pockets, the shoulders and the drape. (3 s) |
| 4 | | Full body from the back. (3 s) |
| 5 | | Full body, settled. Verdict. (3 s) |

- Lines: "Wool, longline, and it still looks sharp open." "The collar does all the work."
- Watch: putting it on, zipping or buttoning happens only across a cut; inside a shot it is
  already on and done up.
- Swap clip: a coat or jacket walk, in a similar length.

## 6. Shoes

Sells on: the shape, the sole, how they look in a step and with the outfit.

Look photo: full body, feet fully in frame on a plain light floor, `2:3`.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full body: steps toward the propped phone, shoes in frame the whole time. Hook line. (3 s) | Same (3 s) |
| 2 | Phone on the floor: the feet step in and stop, one heel lifts. (4 s) | Phone on the floor: step in, one heel lifts. (3 s) |
| 3 | Full body, settled. Verdict. (3 s) | Full body side profile: a walk past the phone. (3 s) |
| 4 | | Three-quarter: one foot forward, the outfit with the shoes. (3 s) |
| 5 | | Full body, settled. Verdict. (3 s) |

- Lines: "Chunky sole, clean white leather." "These go with wide-leg jeans."
- Watch: always a pair, on both feet, both in frame; never held on one palm. A small logo on the
  shoe garbles. The sole's underside shows only if the item photo shows it.
- Swap clip: walking clips with the feet in frame most of the time; similar shoe height (flat
  for flat, heel for heel).

## 7. Bags

Sells on: its size against the body, how it is carried, the hardware.

Look photo: full body, `2:3`, the bag on the shoulder or crossbody, one hand on the strap.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full body: walks past the phone, bag on the shoulder. Hook line. (3 s) | Same (3 s) |
| 2 | Waist-up: one hand on the strap, the hardware turned to the window. (4 s) | Waist-up on the hardware and the leather. (3 s) |
| 3 | Three-quarter: the bag swung to the front. Verdict. (3 s) | Three-quarter: carried in the hand instead (a cut changes how it is carried). (3 s) |
| 4 | | Waist-up: open, the lining showing (opened across the cut). (3 s) |
| 5 | | Full body, settled. Verdict. (3 s) |

- Size: only against the body and only from the item card ("sits at the hip", "fits under the
  arm"). A size given by another object tends to come out too big, so leave objects out of it.
- Lines: "Sits right at the hip." "The gold buckle is the whole point." Name what fits inside only
  if the person said it.
- Watch: one bag in frame; one hand holds it, the other is relaxed; open and closed change only
  across a cut. Things inside are only the ones the person supplied as photos.
- Swap clip: walking or outfit clips with a bag of a similar size carried the same way.

## 8. Jewelry

Sells on: how it catches the light, its size on the body, how it layers.

Look photo: chest-up, `3:4`. Earrings: hair tucked behind the ear. Necklace: an open neckline.
A ring or bracelet: one hand resting near the collarbone.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chest-up: a slow head turn to the window, the earring catching the light. Hook line. (3 s) | Same (3 s) |
| 2 | Chest-up, a little closer: hand to the collarbone, ring and necklace in frame. (4 s) | Chest-up: hand to the collarbone. (3 s) |
| 3 | Chest-up, a small smile. Verdict. (3 s) | Waist-up: the pieces with the outfit. (3 s) |
| 4 | | Chest-up, the other side of the face. (3 s) |
| 5 | | Chest-up, settled. Verdict. (3 s) |

- Lines: "Gold hoops, just the right size." "I'd wear these every day."
- Watch: fine details and engravings never render exactly; put names, materials and prices in
  on-screen text. Never tighter than chest-up. For the most detail, a 9:16 look photo
  (`gpt-image-v2-edit`) animated with `photo-to-video` often beats a multi-shot clip.
- Swap clip: close talking clips, chest-up, hands coming near the face. Small jewelry may not
  read in a swap; say so.

## 9. A full outfit (OOTD)

- The look photo takes up to 4 item photos in `other_elements`, top to bottom; the wardrobe line
  names each piece in the same order.
- 15 s: full body hook / waist-up on the top / the shoes, phone low / the bag or the back / a
  settled verdict.
- One piece per detail shot. Name each piece on screen in Video Studio, one text per shot.
- In the video brief, list every piece in `Wardrobe:` and put each item photo in `objects` in
  the same order (`@Object1` is the first).
- Route B: the Outfit slot takes one image. Make the look photo of the whole outfit first and
  pass it as `outfit`: the swap takes only the clothes from it.

## 10. A reveal, for any item

Template `Before & after`, the avatar's own image as the "before" outfit (no look photo in the
video call).

| Shot | 8 to 12 s |
|---|---|
| 1 | Their everyday clothes, the item on its hanger in one hand, held up to the phone. A short line. (3 to 4 s) |
| 2 | The cut: wearing the item, full body, already moving. The hook's answer. (3 to 4 s) |
| 3 | One detail or a turn, then the verdict. (3 to 4 s) |

- The change happens only on the cut between shots 1 and 2; never dressing on camera.
- Shoes: shot 1 in socks or slippers, pointing down; shot 2 wearing the item.
- Jewelry: a reveal does not read; use a fit check or a look in motion.
