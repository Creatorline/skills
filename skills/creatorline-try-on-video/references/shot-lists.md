# Shot lists by item

What to film for each kind of item, following the arc (try-on-arc.md): the Before shot, the
Wearing reveal, the hands-free detail, the turn and the style pose. Plus the look photo's pose,
sample lines, and what a real clip needs for Route B.

## 1. How to read these

- **Shots.** A 10 s clip holds 3 shots (Before / Wearing / Final look), 15 s holds 5 (Before /
  Wearing / Texture close / Turn / Style pose). Write each shot as one plain line in the brief's
  `Beats:` line, in order, without seconds: the writer times them.
- **Before** is the same for every item: the muted home outfit, the closed kraft bag held by a
  handle, the grin breaking, phone in hand, waist-up (try-on-arc.md, section 4). Only what
  changes per item is listed below.
- **Wearing** always includes one natural turn: a full twirl for clothes and shoes, a head turn
  for jewelry. The words pause while the back is to the camera.
- **Details are hands-free:** one passive cue per detail shot, the second detail a different one.
- **Framing words.** Full body (head to feet), three-quarter (head to knee), waist-up, chest-up,
  close. Neighbouring shots differ in phone, distance and action. The last shot is chest-up or
  wider.
- **Lines.** Opinions, what the frame shows, and the persona's own fit and story ("I'm a medium
  and these fit true to size", "I've worn these all week"). The sample lines are patterns:
  rewrite them from the item card's facts, the brand's voice and the persona.
- **Look photo.** The pose and `aspect_ratio` for the `photo-edit` look. Default model: `2:3` for
  a full body, `3:4` for chest-up.
- **Swap clip.** What a real clip needs for Route B.

## 2. Tops and shirts

Sells on: the neckline, the sleeves, where it sits at the waist (cropped, tucked, oversized), the
drape.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before | Before |
| 2 | Propped, full body in the top with plain trousers, one twirl, settling front. Hook answer. | Same |
| 3 | Propped, three-quarter, a step back, hands in pockets. Verdict. | In hand, close on the neckline and sleeve: a knit catches the light as she breathes. Voice-over. |
| 4 | | Propped, three-quarter from the side: the length at the hip, a glance over the shoulder. |
| 5 | | Another room, full body in a doorframe, settled. Verdict, mid-phrase. |

- Lines: "Boxy fit, cropped right at the waist." "Look at the sleeve on this."
- Watch: a print renders when it is big and simple; small lettering garbles. Tucked or
  untucked, a jacket on or off: one state per shot, changed only across a cut.
- Look photo: three-quarter or full body, `2:3`, plain bottoms named in the wardrobe line.
- Swap clip: a waist-up or full-body try-on in a top of a similar length, arms that move.

## 3. Dresses and skirts

Sells on: the length, the waist, how the hem moves, the back.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before | Before |
| 2 | Propped, full body: one slow twirl, the skirt swinging, half a beat on the back, settling front. | Same |
| 3 | Propped, three-quarter, settled, hands relaxed. Verdict. | In hand, close on the waist: the seam rises and settles with her breath. Voice-over. |
| 4 | | Propped, three-quarter from the back: the ties or the zip, a glance over the shoulder. |
| 5 | | Another room, seated on a window seat or mid-step on the stairs, the hem falling. Verdict, mid-phrase. |

- Lines: "Midi length, right at mid-calf." "The back is the best part." "This is the dress for
  a garden wedding."
- Watch: one twirl only. The length in the wardrobe line so it stays the same in every shot. A
  strapless or backless cut said as it is: "strapless neckline", "open back".
- Look photo: full body, feet in frame, `2:3`.
- Swap clip: a twirl or walk-in dress try-on, in the same length range (mini, midi, maxi).

## 4. Trousers and jeans

Sells on: the rise, the leg shape, the length at the ankle, the movement.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before | Before |
| 2 | Propped, full body with a plain top: one turn, the legs swinging, settling front. | Same |
| 3 | Propped, full body, hands in the pockets, weight on one leg. Verdict. | In hand, pointed down at the legs: the wash fades along the leg as she shifts her weight. Voice-over. |
| 4 | | Propped, three-quarter from the side: the rise and the leg line, a glance back. |
| 5 | | A hallway, mid-step toward the phone, full body. Verdict, mid-phrase. |

- Lines: "High rise, wide leg, cropped at the ankle." "Watch how these move."
- Watch: what the cut does on her is her opinion ("they make my legs look a mile long"), said
  with one place on the body; the top stays the same in every shot.
- Look photo: full body, `2:3`, a plain top and the shoes named in the wardrobe line.
- Swap clip: full-body walking clips; a jeans try-on with turns.

## 5. Outerwear

Sells on: the length, the shoulders, the closure, the collar, how it layers.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before | Before |
| 2 | Propped, full body, already wearing it done up as usual: one turn, the hem swinging out. | Same |
| 3 | Propped, full body, hands in the pockets. Verdict. | In hand, close on the closure: the grain or the weave catches the light as she turns. Voice-over. |
| 4 | | Propped, full body from the back, a look over the shoulder. |
| 5 | | Another room, mid-step through a doorway, settled. Verdict, mid-phrase. |

- Lines: "Wool, longline, and it still looks sharp open." "The collar does all the work."
- Watch: putting it on, zipping or buttoning happens only on the cut; inside a shot it is
  already on and in one state (open or closed, said in the wardrobe line).
- Look photo: full body, `2:3`, worn open or closed as it is usually worn.
- Swap clip: a coat or jacket walk, in a similar length.

## 6. Shoes

Sells on: the shape, the sole, how they look in a step and with the outfit.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before: in hand, pointed down at her feet in plain socks, the kraft bag hanging from her other hand | Same |
| 2 | Propped low, full body, both shoes in frame: a turn on the spot, settling. | Same |
| 3 | Propped, full body, settled, one foot forward. Verdict. | In hand, pointed down at the feet: the leather catches the light as she shifts. Voice-over. |
| 4 | | Propped, full body side profile: a walk past the phone. |
| 5 | | A hallway, three-quarter, one foot forward, the outfit with the shoes. Verdict, mid-phrase. |

- Lines: "Chunky sole, clean white leather." "These go with wide-leg jeans."
- Watch: always a pair, on both feet, both in frame; never held on one palm. A small logo on
  the shoe garbles. The sole's underside shows only if the item photo shows it.
- Look photo: full body, feet fully in frame on a plain light floor, `2:3`.
- Swap clip: walking clips with the feet in frame most of the time; similar shoe height.

## 7. Bags

Sells on: its size against the body, how it is carried, the hardware.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before (a plain outfit; the item is in the kraft bag) | Same |
| 2 | Propped, full body, the bag on the shoulder: a turn, the bag swinging and settling. | Same |
| 3 | Propped, three-quarter: the bag swung to the front, one hand on the strap. Verdict. | In hand, close on the hardware: it catches the light as she turns; her other hand on her hip. Voice-over. |
| 4 | | Propped, three-quarter: carried in the hand instead (changed across the cut). |
| 5 | | Another room, full body, mid-step. Verdict, mid-phrase. |

- Size only against the body and from the item card ("sits at the hip, about 30 cm wide").
- Lines: "Sits right at the hip." "The gold buckle is the whole point." What fits inside is the
  persona's to say ("my laptop and my lunch fit"); a line that says "look" needs a shot that
  shows it.
- Watch: one bag of the item (never two), and the kraft bag never in a shot after the first. One
  hand holds it, the other is relaxed; open and closed change only across a cut.
- Look photo: full body, `2:3`, the bag on the shoulder or crossbody, one hand on the strap.
- Swap clip: walking or outfit clips with a bag of a similar size carried the same way.

## 8. Jewelry

Sells on: how it catches the light, its size on the body, how it layers.

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before, no jewelry, chest-up selfie | Same |
| 2 | Propped, chest-up: a slow head turn to the window, the earring catching the light. | Same |
| 3 | Propped, chest-up, a small smile. Verdict. | In hand, close but no tighter than chest-up: the chain catches the light as she breathes. Voice-over. |
| 4 | | Propped, waist-up: the pieces with the outfit, hand resting at the collarbone. |
| 5 | | Another room by a window, chest-up, the other side of the face. Verdict, mid-phrase. |

- Lines: "Gold hoops, just the right size." "I haven't taken these off all week."
- Watch: fine details and engravings never render exactly; names, materials and prices go in
  on-screen text. Never tighter than chest-up. For the most detail, a 9:16 look photo
  (`gpt-image-v2-edit`) animated with `photo-to-video` often beats a multi-shot clip.
- Look photo: chest-up, `3:4`. Earrings: hair tucked behind the ear. Necklace: a plain crew
  neckline it sits on. A ring or bracelet: one hand resting near the collarbone.
- Swap clip: close talking clips, chest-up, hands coming near the face. Small jewelry may not
  read in a swap; say so.

## 9. A full outfit (OOTD)

- The look photo takes up to 4 item photos in `other_elements`, top to bottom; the wardrobe line
  names each piece in the same order.
- 15 s: Before / full body twirl in the whole outfit / in hand, close on the top's fabric /
  pointed down at the shoes, propped low / another room, settled. One piece per detail shot.
- Name each piece on screen in Video Studio, one text per shot.
- In the video brief, list every piece in `Wardrobe:` and put each item photo in `objects` in
  the same order (`@Object1` is the first).
- Route B: the Outfit slot takes one image. Make the look photo of the whole outfit first and
  pass it as `outfit`: the swap takes only the clothes from it.

## 10. Already wearing it (no Before shot)

The variant for when the person asks to skip the reveal, for a GRWM, and for the locked look
(whose reference already wears the item). The arc starts at Wearing:

| Shot | 10 s | 15 s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Propped, full body: steps back into view already wearing it, the hem still swinging. Hook. | Same |
| 2 | In hand, close, hands-free detail. Voice-over. | Propped, three-quarter: the whole fit at a new distance. |
| 3 | Propped, three-quarter, settled. Verdict. | In hand, close, hands-free detail. Voice-over. |
| 4 | | Propped, from the back, a glance over the shoulder. |
| 5 | | Another room, settled. Verdict, mid-phrase. |

- One twirl, in shot 1 or the back view, not both.
- Jewelry keeps its own chest-up framing (section 8) in this variant too.
