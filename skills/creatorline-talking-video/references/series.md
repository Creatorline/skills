# Series: several talking videos about one product

Use it when the person asks for "a series", "a week of content" or "N posts" of the avatar
talking about one product. A week that mixes formats (a reshoot, a try-on, a talking video) is
`creatorline-content-calendar`. A recurring show with longer episodes is `creatorline-ai-host`.

## The arc table first

Every post is a full paid run, so before anything is made:

1. Show the arc table: one row per post with its number, its shape (story-mode.md, section 3),
   what it does for the viewer, and how hard it sells.
2. Get a nod on the table.
3. Price the whole batch: each post without `confirm`, the total, the balance, one yes
   (house-rules.md, section 2).
4. Run the posts in order. Check each take before the next one starts; a failed post is
   refunded and its retry is a new quote.

## The seven-post skeleton

| Post | Shape | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Staged product reveal | The reveal |
| 2 | Any creator-led shape | The avatar earns attention; the product barely appears |
| 3 | GRWM with stakes | The first soft sell |
| 4 | Guided comparison | Shows how it works against the usual way |
| 5 | Alternate use | A second real use |
| 6 | Mechanism loop | The strongest call to action, in the caption |
| 7 | Behind the concept, or the quirk's payoff | A community post |

- The pressure rises, then eases: light, then selling, then community.
- Fewer posts: keep this order and the same rise and fall.

## What stays the same, word for word

- The avatar: the same one in every post (`avatar_id`), so face and voice carry over.
- The delivery line (monologue-craft.md, section 8).
- The accent line and the quirk line, when there are any.
- The wardrobe line for each place (staging.md, section 6).
- The product card (staging.md, section 10).

## A quirk across the series

The same quirk, unchanged, in posts 1 to 4; noticed by the avatar in post 5; paid off in post 7.

## Repeating it

A series that should run every week is a saved workflow on the same avatar
(`creatorline-workflows`).
