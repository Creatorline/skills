# The brief and the weekly digest

How to write what you found. Short: the person reads the first three lines and decides.

## Contents

- [1. The brief](#1-the-brief)
- [2. The weekly digest](#2-the-weekly-digest)
- [3. Wording](#3-wording)

## 1. The brief

```markdown
**What works for Speakly's competitors** (Weave, Quizlet, Knowt; ads as of the Oct 22 scan)

1. **Weave keeps paying for one talking video.** Its "I failed my exam twice" ad has run
   41 days on Meta, in 6 versions. Their 4 longest-running ads all open on a question to camera.
2. **Knowt's study-hack post did 9 times its usual views** on TikTok (Oct 19), a 7-second
   screen recording with a text hook.
3. **Quizlet launched 5 new ads this week,** all static images with a price in the headline.

My read: a question to camera in the first second is what works in this space; Quizlet is
trying price-led images, too new to say if they work.

Your plan shows 20 of the 189 live ads. [the answer's own line and link]

**Next:**
- Make your version of Weave's 41-day ad with Mia (Reshoot)
- Film Knowt's study-hack post with your avatar (Reshoot)
- Scan for new ads (30 credits, last scan 2 days ago)
```

- The heading names the brand, the competitors and when the ads were read.
- Three numbered items, the most useful first. Each starts with a bold line that says the point,
  then the facts: competitor, platform, the number, the date.
- One line of your reading, marked as yours ("My read:").
- The plan's line only when the answer had one.
- The next steps last, the first one the strongest thing to make.

## 2. The weekly digest

```markdown
**Competitors this week** (Oct 17 to 24, Speakly)

1. **Weave launched 7 new ads,** 5 of them screen recordings of the app.
2. **Knowt's post of Oct 19 did 9 times its usual views.**
3. **Quizlet's 60-day ad is still running.**

- Weave: 7 new ads; best post 2.1 times its usual (Oct 21).
- Quizlet: nothing new this week.
- Knowt: the 9x post above; no ads on Meta or TikTok.

**Next:** [one thing to make, then 1 or 2 more]
```

- Every competitor gets a line, "nothing new this week" included: a missing line reads as
  forgotten.
- The first digest says: "This is the starting point; next week's compares against it."

## 3. Wording

- Plain and neutral: what they did, with its numbers. No judgment of their marketing.
- Their words quoted only briefly, to show a hook ("opens with 'I failed my exam twice'"), never
  a whole script.
- Dates as the person reads them (Oct 19), never as timestamps.
- In the person's language.
